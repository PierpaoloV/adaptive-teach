# Languages domain guide

Track comprehension, production, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and
interaction separately at the level required by the mission. A correct
translation with extensive lookup is different evidence from unaided production.

Use authentic input, controlled production, conversation, reformulation, error
correction, and delayed recall. Vary register, context, and direction of
translation. Record when dictionaries, captions, grammar references, or prompts
were used.

Text-only environments cannot fully verify pronunciation or live listening.
State that limit and use audio evidence or external feedback when available.
