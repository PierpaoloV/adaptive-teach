# Mathematics and physics domain guide

Assess prerequisites at the depth required by the mission: notation, algebra,
geometry, calculus, dimensional reasoning, modeling assumptions, and numerical
methods as applicable.

Separate final result from method, interpretation, and units. Use prediction,
derivation, estimation, counterexamples, limiting cases, and novel problems.
Require the learner to state why a model applies and where it stops applying.

Prefer university curricula, open textbooks, problem sets, and published
solutions used only for verification. Hold back some external problems from
guided practice for milestone assessment.
